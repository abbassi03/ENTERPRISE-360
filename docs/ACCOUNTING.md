# Sample accounting workflows

This document explains the demonstrator's simplified accounting entries. The example chart is illustrative, not a certified Moroccan chart of accounts.

| Event | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Opening bank balance | Bank (1010) | Owner Equity (3000) |
| Opening inventory | Inventory (1200) | Owner Equity (3000) |
| Customer sale | Accounts Receivable (1100) | Sales Revenue (4000), VAT Payable (2100) |
| Cost of goods sold | Cost of Goods Sold (5000) | Inventory (1200) |
| Supplier goods received | Inventory (1200), VAT Recoverable (1400) | Accounts Payable (2000) |
| Customer receipt | Bank (1010) | Accounts Receivable (1100) |
| Supplier payment | Accounts Payable (2000) | Bank (1010) |
| Paid operating expense | Expense account (51xx–59xx) | Bank (1010) |
| Payroll accrual (demo) | Salaries Expense (6000) | Salaries Payable (2200), Payroll Deductions Payable (2110) |

Every journal created by the application is checked for equal debit and credit totals. The demo payroll deduction is a flat illustrative 5%; no statutory Moroccan contribution or tax computation is implemented.
